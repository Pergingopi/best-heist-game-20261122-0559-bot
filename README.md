**The Gilded Vault** — a top-down stealth heist in a museum vault hall at night. You slip in through the south door, cross the hall past three patrolling guards (Rook, Bishop and Knight) and two sweeping wall cameras, pick the vault lock, and slip back out with the loot before the alarm meter fills. Two gems sit on pedestals beside the display cases for the bold. Tools: a smoke pellet (Q) that blinds vision, a noise coin (click) that pulls the nearest guard away to investigate, and a dash (Shift) for the gaps between patrols. Caught at full alarm — or grabbed by a guard — and the run is over.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. WASD or arrow keys to move, Q for smoke, click to toss a coin, Shift to dash, P to pause. `?shot=play|vault|escape|caught&seed=1` stages a screenshot scene instead of starting the game.
